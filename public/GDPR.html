GDPR & DATA PROTECTION STATEMENT

MYSHOPSWIFT LTD

Last Updated: 8 November 2026

1. Introduction

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), together with the UK Data Protection Act 2018, requires organisations to be transparent about the personal information they collect, why they collect it, how it is used and with whom it may be shared.

MyShopSwift Ltd ("MyShopSwift", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy and confidentiality of its customers and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and securely.

As an online retailer providing African and Caribbean groceries, food products, household essentials and everyday products, we collect and process personal information to operate our website, manage customer accounts, process orders, arrange deliveries and provide customer support.

We act as the data controller for the personal information we collect and process in connection with our business activities.

This statement explains how we process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation.

2. Collection and Processing of Personal Information

You may provide your personal information directly to us when you:

  • Register or create an account on our website.
  • Place an order or purchase products.
  • Provide delivery details.
  • Contact us through our website or email.
  • Participate in our customer rewards programme.
  • Communicate with our customer support team.

We may also automatically collect certain technical information when you access or use our website, including your IP address, browser information, device details and website activity.

We process this information for the purposes of providing our online shopping services, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing, confirming and fulfilling customer orders.
  • Arranging deliveries and providing order updates.
  • Processing payments and managing refunds.
  • Administering customer rewards and loyalty points.
  • Responding to customer enquiries and complaints.
  • Maintaining accurate business and transaction records.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities and protecting our website.
  • Improving our products, services and customer shopping experience.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, MyShopSwift must have a lawful basis for collecting and processing personal information.

Depending on the purpose, we rely on the following lawful bases:

A. Contractual Necessity

We process personal information where necessary to fulfil our contractual obligations to customers, including managing accounts, processing purchases, arranging deliveries and administering payments.

B. Legitimate Interests

We may process personal information where necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these interests do not override the rights and freedoms of individuals.

These interests include managing our business, improving our website, maintaining customer relationships, preventing fraud and ensuring the security of our services.

C. Legal Obligations

We may process personal information where necessary to comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements, including tax, accounting and financial record-keeping obligations.

D. Consent

Where required, we will obtain your consent before processing personal information for specific purposes, including certain marketing communications and the use of non-essential cookies.

You may withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Sharing of Personal Information

MyShopSwift does not sell or rent customer personal information to third parties.

However, we may share relevant personal information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our business and deliver our services.

These may include:

  • Payment processors, including Stripe, for secure payment transactions.
  • Website hosting and technology providers.
  • Delivery personnel and authorised fulfilment partners.
  • Customer service and communication providers.
  • Accountants, legal advisers and professional consultants.
  • Government authorities and regulatory organisations where legally required.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose.

5. Data Security and Confidentiality

MyShopSwift takes appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures include secure password hashing, protected customer login sessions, restricted access to customer records and the use of established third-party payment processing services.

We ensure that access to customer information is limited to authorised individuals who require it to perform their responsibilities.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected or to comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Customer account details, order histories, payment records and customer communications may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose and applicable legal requirements.

When personal information is no longer required, we take appropriate steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable UK data protection legislation, you have rights concerning your personal information.

These include the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is collected and used.
  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Request restrictions on how your information is processed.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request the transfer of certain personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise these rights by contacting MyShopSwift through our website or by emailing us.

Please note that certain information may need to be retained where we have a legal obligation or another lawful basis to do so.

8. Marketing and Customer Communications

We may use customer contact details to provide essential communications relating to orders, payments, deliveries and account activities.

Where required, we will obtain your consent before sending promotional emails, text messages or other direct marketing communications.

You can unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website uses essential cookies and similar technologies to maintain website functionality, secure customer sessions and support the online shopping experience.

Where non-essential cookies are introduced, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent where required.

Further information is available in our Privacy page

10. International Data Transfers

Where our service providers process or access personal information outside the United Kingdom, MyShopSwift will ensure that appropriate legal safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

11. Complaints and Further Information

If you have any questions about how MyShopSwift collects, uses or protects your personal information, or if you wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us.
