About MyShopSwift

Shop easy. Order fast. Delivered swift.

Who we are

MyShopSwift is your online destination for everyday groceries, lifestyle essentials and more. We bring together British, African and Caribbean food products, household provisions, gadgets and accessories in one convenient place.
Our mission is simple: to make shopping easier by giving households access to the products they love, at fair prices, with convenient and reliable delivery, supported by smart technology and AI-powered assistance.

What we offer

From fresh meat and fish, spices, condiments, drinks, fresh produce, grains and everyday cupboard essentials to ready meals, beauty products, household essentials and everyday gadgets, MyShopSwift brings together a diverse range of products to meet your everyday needs. Whether you're shopping for familiar favourites, exploring new flavours or stocking up on household essentials, you can find what you need in one place, with prices clearly displayed in pounds sterling.

Our promise

  • At MyShopSwift, customer convenience and satisfaction come first.
  • Transparent Pricing: Clear, competitive prices with no hidden charges.
  • Convenient Delivery: Reliable delivery options, with free delivery on qualifying orders over £100.
  • Flexible Payments: Pay by card, Cash on Delivery or eligible MyShopSwift reward points.
  • AI-Powered Assistance: Get smart, helpful assistance with product enquiries, shopping guidance and customer support whenever you need it.
  • Customer Support: Friendly, responsive human support when you need further assistance.

Get in touch

We're always happy to hear from you. Whether you have a question, feedback, or a business partnership enquiry, our team is ready to help. Visit our Contact Us page.
