About MyShopSwift

Who we are

MyShopSwift is an online grocery and lifestyle shop bringing together English and Afro-Caribbean groceries, everyday provisions, and gadgets and accessories — all in one place, delivered across the UK.

What we offer

From fresh meat and fish, condiments and spices, drinks, fruit and vegetables, grains and cupboard staples, to ready meals, beauty and household essentials, and everyday gadgets — we've built our catalogue around what households actually shop for every week, priced fairly in pounds sterling.

Our promise

  • Straightforward, transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • Fast, reliable delivery nationwide, free on orders over £100
  • Flexible payment — card, Cash on Delivery, or your MyShopSwift reward points
  • Real customer support when you need it
