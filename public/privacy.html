Privacy Policy

How MyShopSwift collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password — we never store passwords in plain text. When you place an order, we collect your delivery address and the details of what you've ordered. If you contact us, we keep the message and the email address you provided so we can reply.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in between visits
  • To process, fulfil, and let you track your orders
  • To operate our rewards/points programme and apply your points to orders
  • To respond to enquiries sent through our Contact Us form
  • To improve our products, service, and website

Accounts & sessions

Your login session is kept using a secure, HTTP-only cookie. This cookie can't be read by JavaScript and is only used to recognise you as signed in — it doesn't track you across other websites.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant third-party payment processor. MyShopSwift never sees or stores your full card number. Cash on Delivery orders and reward-points payments are handled entirely within our own systems.

Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies — primarily the session cookie that keeps you signed in. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Data protection

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, including password hashing, secure session cookies, and restricting access to customer and order data to what's needed to run the shop. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

Your rights

You can review your account details, orders, and rewards at any time by signing in. If you'd like your data corrected or removed, or have any other privacy question, please contact us.
