Privacy Policy

How MyShopSwift collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: 8 November 2026

1. Introduction

MyShopSwift Ltd ("MyShopSwift", "we", "our" or "us") is an online retail business providing African and Caribbean groceries, food products, ousehold essentials and other everyday items to customers across the United Kingdom.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely, responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, protect and share your personal information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase products, use our services or communicate with us.

MyShopSwift Ltd acts as the data controller in respect of the personal information we collect and process.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), where applicable.

By using our website and services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Notice.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process different categories of personal information depending on how you interact with MyShopSwift.

A. Personal and Account Information

When you create an account or register on our website, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Account login details.
  • Securely hashed password information.
  • Account preferences and registration details.

We do not store passwords in plain text.

B. Order and Delivery Information

When you place an order through our website, we may collect:

  • Delivery address and postcode.
  • Contact telephone number, where provided.
  • Details of products purchased.
  • Order reference and transaction history.
  • Delivery instructions and preferences.
  • Order status and delivery confirmation information.

This information enables us to process, prepare, fulfil and deliver your orders.

C. Payment Information

When you make a purchase, we collect information relating to your payment method and transaction.

For online card payments, transactions are processed securely through Stripe, our third-party payment processor.

MyShopSwift does not collect or store customers' complete card numbers or card security codes on its own systems.

For Cash on Delivery transactions and payments made using eligible reward points, relevant payment and order records are maintained within our systems.

D. Communication Information

If you contact us through our Contact Us form, email or other available communication channels, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address.
  • Message or enquiry details.
  • Any additional information you voluntarily provide.
  • Delivery instructions and preferences.

We retain this information to respond to your enquiries and maintain appropriate customer service records.

E. Website and Technical Information

When you access our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device information.
  • Website access times.
  • Pages visited and general website activity.

This information may be used to maintain website security, troubleshoot technical problems and improve website performance.

3. Purpose of Processing and Legal Basis

We collect and process personal information for specific business purposes and ensure that we have an appropriate lawful basis under the UK GDPR.

The purposes and legal bases include:

A. Contractual Necessity

We process personal information where it is necessary to fulfil our contractual obligations to you, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing and confirming orders.
  • Preparing and delivering purchased products.
  • Managing payments and refunds.
  • Providing order updates and delivery information.
  • Administering customer reward points.

Without certain information, we may be unable to accept or fulfil your order.

B. Legitimate Interests

We may process personal information where it is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.

These interests include:

  • Maintaining and improving our website.
  • Managing customer relationships.
  • Preventing fraudulent or suspicious transactions.
  • Managing payments and refunds.
  • Protecting our business, customers and systems.
  • Responding to customer enquiries.
  • Maintaining appropriate business and operational records.
  • Improving our products and services.

C. Legal Obligations

We may process and retain personal information where necessary to comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including:

  • Tax and accounting requirements.
  • Financial record-keeping obligations.
  • Consumer protection legislation.
  • Responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities.
  • Meeting applicable legal and reporting requirements.

D. Consent

Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before processing personal information for specific purposes.

For example, we will seek your consent before sending electronic marketing communications where consent is the appropriate legal basis.

You may withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Legitimate Interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis for processing personal information, our interests include:

  • Operating and managing an efficient online grocery retail business.
  • Providing customers with a reliable shopping and delivery experience.
  • Maintaining accurate customer and order records.
  • Preventing misuse, fraud and unauthorised access.
  • Improving our website, products and customer experience.
  • Maintaining communication with existing customers regarding their orders and enquiries.
  • Protecting our commercial interests and business operations.

We consider the impact of our processing on customers' privacy rights and ensure that personal information is only used where reasonably necessary.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Information

MyShopSwift does not sell, rent or trade customers' personal information to third parties.

However, we may share relevant information with trusted third-party service providers where necessary to operate our business and provide our services.

These may include:

A. Payment Service Providers

We use Stripe to securely process online card payments and manage relevant payment transactions.

Payment information is handled in accordance with Stripe's applicable privacy and security arrangements.

B. Website Hosting and Technology Providers

Our website domain and associated business services are managed through GoDaddy.

We may rely on website hosting, email, technical support and other technology providers to maintain our online services.

C. Delivery and Fulfilment Services

Relevant customer information, including delivery addresses, contact details and order information, may be accessed by authorised individuals responsible for preparing, handling and delivering customer orders.

This is necessary to ensure that orders reach the correct customers.

D. Professional and Business Advisers

We may share relevant information with professional advisers, including accountants, legal advisers, insurers and other professional service providers, where reasonably necessary for business operations or legal compliance.

E. Government and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose personal information where required by law, regulation, court order or a legitimate request from an authorised public authority.

We ensure that any information shared is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Data Retention

MyShopSwift will retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying legal, accounting and regulatory requirements.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods.

Our general approach is as follows:

  • Customer account information: Retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • Order and transaction records: Retained for the periods required by applicable tax, accounting and financial record-keeping legislation.
  • Payment records: Retained as necessary for financial reconciliation, fraud prevention and legal compliance.
  • Customer enquiries: Retained for a reasonable period after the enquiry has been resolved.
  • Technical and website information: Retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting and website administration.
  • Rewards information: Retained while your rewards account remains active and for any additional period necessary to resolve outstanding transactions or meet legal obligations.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable UK data protection legislation, you have several rights concerning your personal information.

These include:

A. Right to Be Informed

You have the right to understand how and why we collect and use your personal information.

B. Right of Access

You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

C. Right to Rectification

You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

D. Right to Erasure

You may request the deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.

E. Right to Restrict Processing

You may ask us to restrict how we use your personal information in certain circumstances.

F. Right to Data Portability

You may request a copy of certain personal information in a commonly used, machine-readable format where the right applies.

G. Right to Object

You have the right to object to certain types of personal information processing, particularly where we rely on legitimate interests.

You have an absolute right to object to the use of your personal information for direct marketing.

H. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on your consent to process personal information, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Exercising these rights will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before your withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in the Contact Information section of this notice.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to support essential website functionality and improve the shopping experience.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

We primarily use essential cookies for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining secure customer login sessions.
  • Recognising customers who are signed into their accounts.
  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Maintaining shopping basket functionality, where applicable.
  • Supporting website security.

Our secure session cookies are configured to prevent unauthorised access through JavaScript where supported.

We do not use third-party advertising or behavioural tracking cookies as part of our stated website functionality.

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent where required by applicable law.

9. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against accidental loss, destruction, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

Our security measures include:

  • Secure password hashing.
  • Secure website session management.
  • HTTP-only cookies for customer authentication.
  • Restricted access to customer and order information.
  • Secure third-party payment processing.
  • Appropriate access controls for business systems.
  • Reasonable measures to protect our website and associated technology infrastructure.

Access to personal information is restricted to authorised individuals who require the information to perform their business responsibilities.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security but remain committed to maintaining appropriate safeguards.

10. International Transfers of Personal Information

Some of the third-party service providers we use may operate internationally or store and process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

These safeguards may include:

  • Transfers to countries recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection.
  • Appropriate contractual safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, where applicable.
  • Other legally recognised transfer mechanisms.

Where required, further information about relevant international transfers and safeguards can be requested by contacting us.

11. Marketing Communications

We may occasionally wish to inform customers about MyShopSwift products, promotions, special offers, new arrivals and other relevant business updates.

. Where required, we will obtain your consent before sending marketing communications by email or text message.

You can withdraw your consent or unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the unsubscribe link included in our marketing emails, where available.
  • Contacting us directly through our website.
  • Sending an email to our customer support address.

Please note that withdrawing marketing consent will not prevent us from sending essential service communications relating to your account, purchases, payments or deliveries.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, payment platforms or other third-party services.

Please be aware that MyShopSwift does not control the privacy practices, security arrangements or content of external websites.

When you access an external website, we encourage you to review its privacy notice and terms of use.

This Privacy Notice applies only to personal information collected and processed by MyShopSwift.

13. Business Transfers

If MyShopSwift Ltd undergoes a business sale, merger, restructuring, acquisition or transfer of ownership, customer information may be disclosed to relevant professional advisers, prospective purchasers or successor organisations where necessary.

Any transfer of personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

Where appropriate, customers will be informed of any material changes affecting how their personal information is processed.

14. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals who are legally able to enter into purchases and transactions.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian authorisation is legally required.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to investigate and address the matter.

15. Changes to This Privacy Notice

MyShopSwift may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our business operations, website functionality, technology, legal requirements or data protection practices.

Any updates will be published on our website, together with the revised date.

Where significant changes affect how we process personal information, we will take appropriate steps to inform affected customers.

We encourage customers to review this Privacy Notice periodically.
